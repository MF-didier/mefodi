Who We Are?

MEFODI SIGN LLC specializes in the manufacture of visual communication supports and professional signage solutions. We transform ideas and concepts into durable physical structures that help businesses stand out with strong visual identity. Our expertise is built on laser cutting precision, innovative materials, and modern fabrication techniques. From luxury 3D signs and Neon Flex creations to road signage and custom branding solutions, we deliver high-quality products designed for durability and impact.

Create stunning visuals with as much or as little control as you want thanks to our Basic and Creative modes.

MEFODI SIGN gives you the freedom to design, illuminate, and transform your ideas into spectacular signage.

  • Indoor & Outdoor Signage for shops, offices, and events
